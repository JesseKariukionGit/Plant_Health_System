📖 About This System

🌱 Plant Health Diagnosis & Recommendation

This AI-powered tool helps you identify plant diseases from leaf images and provides safe, actionable treatment recommendations.

🔬 How It Works

1. Upload — Take a photo of the affected leaf and upload it.
2. AI Analysis — Our deep learning model analyzes the image and identifies the disease.
3. Treatment — You receive a detailed treatment plan based on verified agricultural sources.

🧪 Technology

  • CNN Classifier: EfficientNet-B0 trained on PlantVillage (38 classes, 88% accuracy).
  • RAG System: Retrieves treatment information from university extension guides.
  • Safety First: All advice includes a mandatory disclaimer and confidence threshold.

👨‍🌾 Intended Use

This system is designed for educational and research purposes. It is not a substitute for professional agronomic advice. Always verify treatments with a local expert before application.
